What engineers need

Sensors measure the air. People protect it.

Communication & listening

Explaining problems clearly and listening carefully, because everyone involved sees the problem a little differently.

Hira talks with the manager and residents.

Honesty & integrity

People trust engineers to tell the truth about the air they breathe: check the facts, report them accurately, and never hide problems.

Hira checks the sensor before acting.

Adaptability

Air quality can change without warning, so engineers must be ready to change their plans when something new happens.

Hira plans extra monitoring.
